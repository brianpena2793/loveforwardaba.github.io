HIPAA Notice of Privacy Practices

This notice describes how medical information about you may be used and disclosed and how you can get access to this information. Please review it carefully.

Who this notice covers

This notice applies to protected health information held by Love Forward ABA in providing behavior analytic services. “You” includes a parent, guardian, or other personal representative acting within their legal authority. We verify that authority and follow applicable rules for minors’ confidential care.

Your rights

  • Access: Request to inspect or receive an electronic or paper copy of your clinical and billing records. We generally respond within 30 days; if a lawful extension or denial applies, we explain it in writing. Only lawful, reasonable, cost-based copy fees may apply. A shorter state deadline controls where applicable.
  • Corrections: Ask us in writing to amend information you believe is wrong or incomplete. We generally respond within 60 days, explain any lawful extension or denial, and explain your right to submit a disagreement.
  • Confidential communications: Request a particular contact method or mailing address. We accommodate reasonable requests.
  • Limits on sharing: Request limits on uses or disclosures for treatment, payment, or operations. We generally do not have to agree. However, we must honor a qualifying request not to disclose a service to your health plan for payment or operations when you paid for that service in full out of pocket, unless disclosure is required by law.
  • Disclosure accounting: Request a list of certain disclosures made in the preceding six years. Treatment, payment, operations, authorized disclosures, and other legal exceptions are excluded. One accounting in a 12-month period is free; we tell you about any lawful fee for additional requests.
  • A copy and a representative: Request a paper copy of this notice even if you received it electronically. A legally authorized representative may exercise your rights, subject to applicable law.

How information may be used or shared

  • Treatment: To assess needs, develop and deliver ABA treatment, supervise care, and coordinate with treating professionals. For example, a BCBA may review progress with your treating clinician.
  • Payment: To request authorizations, bill for services, and obtain payment. For example, an insurer may receive assessment information supporting a service request.
  • Health care operations: To manage services, review quality, train staff, and meet compliance responsibilities. Service providers handling information on our behalf must have required safeguards and agreements.
  • Other permitted or required purposes: Subject to applicable legal conditions, disclosures may support public health; reporting suspected abuse, neglect, or domestic violence; preventing a serious and imminent threat; health oversight; legally authorized research; workers’ compensation; certain law-enforcement or government functions; organ donation; or coroners and medical examiners. We may disclose information required by law, including to HHS for compliance review, and respond to qualifying court orders or other legal process. A request or subpoena alone does not automatically authorize release.

We follow stronger state and federal protections where they apply. Mental health records, minors’ records, and other specially protected information may require additional permission or legal authority beyond HIPAA’s general permissions. The final notice must describe applicable state-specific restrictions before use.

Your choices and written permission

You may agree or object to appropriate sharing with people involved in your care or payment and for disaster relief. If you cannot express a preference, we may use professional judgment within legal limits to act in your best interest.

Uses and disclosures outside this notice generally require your written authorization. Marketing uses requiring authorization, sale of protected health information, and most disclosures of separately maintained psychotherapy notes require written authorization unless a specific legal exception applies. Routine ABA session notes are not automatically psychotherapy notes. You may revoke authorization in writing; revocation does not undo actions already taken in reliance on it.

If fundraising communications are used, you may opt out. If we hold substance use disorder records protected by 42 CFR Part 2, additional restrictions apply: they cannot be used or disclosed in proceedings against you without your written consent or a qualifying court order and subpoena. Any fundraising using those records requires advance notice and a choice about receiving those communications.

Our responsibilities

We must protect your health information, follow the notice currently in effect, provide copies, and notify affected individuals of breaches of unsecured protected health information as required by law. We may revise this notice and apply lawful changes to information already held. An updated notice will be posted here and made available on request.
