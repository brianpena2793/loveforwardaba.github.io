Website Privacy Policy

Effective: October 22, 2026

Information we collect and use

Love Forward ABA uses contact details and message contents you provide to answer inquiries and coordinate services. GitHub Pages, our hosting provider, processes IP addresses and request data to deliver and protect this site. Google Analytics uses cookies and similar technologies on informational pages to collect online identifiers, device/browser details, referral information, pages visited, and interactions so we can understand website use. Analytics is excluded from Get Started and privacy pages. Browser local storage remembers your language preference.

Sharing and intake

Hosting and analytics providers process website data for those services. We may disclose information as required by law or to address security issues. Third-party analytics providers may collect information about your activity over time and across websites. Intake links open Alpaca Health, which processes contact, insurance, and health information under its applicable notices. Avoid sending sensitive clinical information through general email; request secure sharing instructions.

Your choices and privacy contact

Manage cookies and local storage in your browser or use Google’s Analytics opt-out tool. This site does not respond to Do Not Track signals. To review information you provided, request corrections or deletion, or ask about clinical privacy, contact our Privacy Officer at privacy@loveforwardaba.com. We may verify your identity; legal retention requirements may limit deletion.

Scope and changes

This policy covers our public website and does not replace our HIPAA notice. The site is intended for adults; contact us if a child submits personal information. We post policy changes here with a revised effective date.
