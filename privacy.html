Website Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 22, 2026

About this policy

This policy explains information handling on Love Forward ABA’s public website. Clinical information is covered separately by our . Contact us at info@loveforwardaba.com with website privacy questions.

Information collected through the website

  • Information you choose to share: When you email us, we receive your email address and whatever information you include. We use it to respond to your inquiry, discuss services, and coordinate next steps. Please avoid sending sensitive clinical information through general email.
  • Technical information: GitHub Pages hosts this website. Hosting providers may process IP addresses and other request information to deliver and protect the site.
  • Analytics: Our informational pages use Google Analytics, which may collect online identifiers, browser and device information, pages visited, referral information, and interactions through cookies and similar technology. We use analytics to understand website use. Google Analytics is not included on our Get Started or privacy pages.
  • Language preference: Your browser’s local storage saves your English or Spanish selection under “lfaba-language.” This helps open pages in your selected language.

Intake and external services

Our intake links open Alpaca Health on a separate website. Information entered there, including contact, insurance, or health information, is submitted through that service rather than a form hosted on this public site. Review the notices presented by Alpaca before submitting information. Contact us if you need another way to begin intake.

External services have their own privacy and security practices. Our website providers process information needed to host the site or provide analytics; we may also disclose information when required by law or to address security issues. A link to another site does not mean that its settings or practices are identical to ours.

Your choices and requests

You can manage or delete cookies and local storage in your browser, use browser tracking controls, or use Google’s Analytics opt-out browser add-on. Clearing local storage resets the saved language preference. This site does not currently change its behavior in response to a browser Do Not Track signal. Third-party analytics providers may collect information about activity across websites according to their settings and policies.

To ask about personal information you have shared, request a correction or deletion, or ask about available privacy rights, email us. We may need to verify your identity, and legal or clinical record-retention duties may limit deletion. This policy does not promise deletion of records that must be retained.

Children, security, and updates

This website is intended for caregivers, referral sources, and professionals. Children should not submit personal information directly. If you believe a child submitted information, contact us so we can review it.

The site uses HTTPS. This does not make ordinary email a secure clinical messaging system. Use the intake service or ask our team for secure sharing instructions. Website information is educational and is not emergency care or an individualized treatment plan.

We may update this policy as the site or our practices change. The date above identifies the latest revision.
