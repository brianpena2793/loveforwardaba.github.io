Brian Peña, M.Ed., BCBA, LBA

Co-Founder & Clinical Director

Hi, I'm Brian, a Board Certified Behavior Analyst (BCBA) and co-founder of Love Forward ABA Company. At the heart of everything I do is a simple belief: every learner deserves patience, creativity, and genuine care, and every family deserves to feel confident and supported along the way. I'm especially passionate about early intervention, family collaboration, and walking alongside Spanish-speaking and first-generation families as they navigate this journey. My path into ABA started back in 2014, almost by chance. I was searching for a career that fit my psychology degree when I stumbled upon a position teaching children through fun, playful, engaging activities, and as someone who'd always dreamed of being an educator, I was hooked immediately. The more I worked with different learners, the more I fell in love with figuring out what made each child light up, and adapting my approach to fit their unique strengths and interests. That joy of finding creative ways to help each child succeed is what turned a job into a genuine calling. I earned my Bachelor's degree in Psychology from California State University, Fullerton (CSUF), and later my Master's in Education, with an emphasis in Educational Technology, also at CSUF, while completing my ABA coursework through UMass Global. That season of learning brought together everything I care about: education, thoughtful instructional design, and behavior analysis. I became a BCBA in 2023 and am proudly licensed in both Arizona and Georgia. These days, my heart is in early intervention with children ages 2 to 8, and in working closely with caregivers so they feel truly equipped- not just handed strategies, but supported in making them work in the beautiful chaos of everyday life. That same care for building something sustainable led me to co-found LoveForward ABA Company, where I help shape systems and operations that keep quality, consistent, compassionate ABA care at the center of everything we do. Outside of work, I'm a Los Angeles native who grew up in Long Beach, CA. My wife and I made the leap to the Central Valley (Fresno area) in 2021, and we've loved trading the city for mountain views, with our Australian Shepherd, Ranger, always by our side.
