Brian Peña, M.Ed., BCBA, LBA

Co-Founder & Clinical Director

Hi, I'm Brian, a Board Certified Behavior Analyst (BCBA) and co-founder of Love Forward ABA Company. At the heart of everything I do is a simple belief: every learner deserves patience, creativity, and genuine care, and every family deserves to feel confident and supported along the way.

I'm especially passionate about early intervention, family collaboration, and walking alongside Spanish-speaking and first-generation families as they navigate their ABA journey.

My path into ABA started in 2014 while I was searching for a career that connected with my background in psychology. I came across a position focused on teaching children through fun, playful, and engaging activities. As someone who had always wanted to be an educator, I quickly found a passion for discovering what motivates each learner and adapting my approach around their individual strengths and interests.

I earned my Bachelor's degree in Psychology from California State University, Fullerton and later completed my Master's degree in Education with an emphasis in Educational Technology, also at CSUF. At the same time, I completed my ABA coursework through UMass Global. I became a Board Certified Behavior Analyst in 2023 and later became licensed in Arizona and Georgia.

Today, much of my clinical focus is in early intervention with children ages 2 to 8. I also enjoy working closely with caregivers so strategies feel practical, meaningful, and sustainable within everyday family life.

That same passion for building sustainable systems led me to co-found Love Forward ABA, where I also focus on developing organizational systems and operations that support consistent, compassionate, and high-quality ABA care.

Outside of work, I'm a Los Angeles native who grew up in Long Beach. My wife and I moved to California's Central Valley in 2021, where we've enjoyed trading the city for mountain views with our Australian Shepherd, Ranger, always nearby.
