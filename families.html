Starting ABA doesn't have to feel overwhelming.

We want families to understand what comes next and feel supported throughout the process.

02

03

Assessment & planning

When appropriate, the clinical team completes an assessment and develops an individualized treatment plan.

04

Begin services

Once services are approved and the team is in place, we begin working together toward meaningful goals.

Common questions

What can I ask?

Families often have questions about eligibility, insurance, scheduling, treatment goals, caregiver participation, and what sessions look like.

We encourage you to ask questions before getting started. There is no need to have everything figured out before reaching out.

Privacy reminder

Please do not send sensitive medical or behavioral information through a regular website contact form. We can provide a secure way to share information during the intake process.
